# Midjourney 提示词模板 — 巴里·詹金斯 Barry Jenkins 风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

---

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Barry Jenkins, saturated warm brown skin,
twilight purple-blue palette, tender intimacy,
urban poetry, soft shallow focus,
cinematic film look, anamorphic lens
```

## 二、场景模板（6类）
### 模板A：暮光城市
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Barry Jenkins, twilight purple-blue night city, warm brown skin tones, tender intimacy, streetlamp glow --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 750 --v 6`
### 模板B：人物肖像
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Barry Jenkins, centered portrait, saturated warm skin, soft shallow focus, looking at camera --ar 3:4 --style raw --s 600 --v 6`
### 模板C：海滩日光
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Barry Jenkins, golden hour beach, warm golden brown skin, teal ocean, poetic atmosphere --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 800 --v 6`
### 模板D：爱情双人
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Barry Jenkins, tender couple, circular composition, warm tungsten streetlight, slow dance --ar 21:9 --style raw --s 700 --v 6`
### 模板E：成长回忆
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Barry Jenkins, childhood memory, dreamlike soft focus, purple-blue twilight, coming of age --ar 1:1 --style raw --s 900 --v 6`
### 模板F：社会正义
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Barry Jenkins, racial justice story, warm brown skin, deep emotion, urban setting --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 650 --v 6`

## 三、负面提示词
`--no cold flat lighting, desaturated skin, cartoon anime, overexposed, harsh contrast, stereotype`

## 四、参数速查
| 效果 | 参数 |
|------|------|
| 电影感 | `--s 750 --style raw --v 6` |
| 写实 | `--s 250 --style raw --v 6` |
| 艺术化 | `--s 900 --v 6` |
| 宽银幕 | `--ar 21:9` |
| 肖像 | `--ar 3:4` |
