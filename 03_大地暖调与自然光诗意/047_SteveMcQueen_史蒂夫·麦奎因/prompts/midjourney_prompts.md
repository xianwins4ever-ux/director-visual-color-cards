# Midjourney 提示词模板 — 史蒂夫·麦奎因 Steve McQueen 风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

---

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Steve McQueen, painterly warm brown palette,
historical weight, natural available light,
ochre and umber tones, long take stillness,
human dignity and suffering, cinematic film look
```

## 二、场景模板（6类）
### 模板A：历史油画
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Steve McQueen, historical painterly scene, deep brown and ochre palette, warm natural light, solemn dignity --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 750 --v 6`
### 模板B：人物肖像
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Steve McQueen, intimate portrait, warm brown skin tones, painterly side lighting, quiet endurance --ar 3:4 --style raw --s 600 --v 6`
### 模板C：都市疏离
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Steve McQueen, urban alienation, earthy red-brown and cold steel grey, shallow depth of field --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 800 --v 6`
### 模板D：政治抗争
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Steve McQueen, political resistance, prison or protest scene, olive drab and ochre, visceral intensity --ar 21:9 --style raw --s 700 --v 6`
### 模板E：女性力量
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Steve McQueen, women of color heist crew, warm brown black and dark gold, nocturnal tension --ar 1:1 --style raw --s 900 --v 6`
### 模板F：集体记忆
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Steve McQueen, community historical scene, warm earth gold palette, long static take, collective memory --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 650 --v 6`

## 三、负面提示词
`--no bright saturated, neon cyberpunk, cartoon anime, romanticized violence, melodramatic, oversaturated`

## 四、参数速查
| 效果 | 参数 |
|------|------|
| 电影感 | `--s 750 --style raw --v 6` |
| 写实 | `--s 250 --style raw --v 6` |
| 艺术化 | `--s 900 --v 6` |
| 宽银幕 | `--ar 21:9` |
| 肖像 | `--ar 3:4` |
