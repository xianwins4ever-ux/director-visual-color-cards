# Midjourney 提示词模板 — 伊纳里图 Alejandro G. Iñárritu 风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

---

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Alejandro G. Iñárritu, long take natural light,
mud and blood warm tones, handheld immersive camera,
visceral physicality, existential weight,
warm earth and cold winter palette, cinematic film look
```
