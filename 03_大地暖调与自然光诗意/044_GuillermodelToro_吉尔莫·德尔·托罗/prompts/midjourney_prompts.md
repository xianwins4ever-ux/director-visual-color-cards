# Midjourney 提示词模板 — 吉尔莫·德尔·托罗 Guillermo del Toro 风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Guillermo del Toro, amber fairy tale palette,
warm black gothic atmosphere, dark gold mythic tones,
sympathetic creature design, magical realism,
candlelit warm lighting, cinematic film look
```
