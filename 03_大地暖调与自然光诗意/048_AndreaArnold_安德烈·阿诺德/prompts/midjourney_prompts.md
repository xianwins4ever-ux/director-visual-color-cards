# Midjourney 提示词模板 — Andrea Arnold 安德烈·阿诺德风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

---

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Andrea Arnold, british social realism,
handheld natural light, golden hour amber glow,
earth-tone color palette, cinéma vérité documentary,
working class intimacy, raw cinematic film look
```

## 二、场景模板（6类）
### 模板A：黄昏户外
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Andrea Arnold, golden hour natural light, british council estate, handheld cinéma vérité, earthy color palette --ar 4:3 --style raw --s 250 --v 6`
### 模板B：人物肖像
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Andrea Arnold, intimate close-up portrait, natural side light, skin texture detail, working class setting --ar 3:4 --style raw --s 200 --v 6`
### 模板C：荒野旷野
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Andrea Arnold, windswept moorland, heather green and brown earth tones, overcast natural light, raw natural beauty --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 300 --v 6`
### 模板D：公路旅途
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Andrea Arnold, american road trip, sunset golden light, van interior, transient youth freedom --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250 --v 6`
### 模板E：室内纪实
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Andrea Arnold, dimly lit council flat, natural window light, lived-in clutter, intimate documentary feel --ar 4:3 --style raw --s 200 --v 6`
### 模板F：动物隐喻
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Andrea Arnold, animal in natural setting, horse on moor or white horse at dusk, symbolic naturalism, golden hour --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 300 --v 6`

## 三、负面提示词
`--no studio lighting, glossy commercial look, perfect makeup, overly saturated colors, steady tripod shot`

## 四、参数速查
| 效果 | 参数 |
|------|------|
| 纪实感 | `--s 200-250 --style raw --v 6` |
| 诗意化 | `--s 400-500 --style raw --v 6` |
| 4:3画幅 | `--ar 4:3` |
| 宽银幕 | `--ar 16:9` |
| 肖像 | `--ar 3:4` |
