# Midjourney 提示词模板 — Chloé Zhao 赵婷风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

---

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Chloe Zhao, nomadland aesthetic,
natural magic hour light, dusty earth color palette,
wide open landscape, quiet human dignity,
cinematic film still, meditative stillness
```

## 二、场景模板（6类）
### 模板A：旷野公路
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Chloe Zhao, wide open desert landscape, magic hour golden light, dusty earth palette, horizon composition, nomadic life --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 200 --v 6`
### 模板B：人物肖像
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Chloe Zhao, intimate natural light portrait, shallow depth of field, muted warm skin tone, weathered face, quiet dignity --ar 3:4 --style raw --s 180 --v 6`
### 模板C：营地黄昏
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Chloe Zhao, nomad camp at golden hour, rv vans in desert, warm soft light, community of wanderers --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250 --v 6`
### 模板D：草原骑马
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Chloe Zhao, vast prairie landscape, horse rider at golden hour, sagebrush and dusty earth, meditative western --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250 --v 6`
### 模板E：神话史诗
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Chloe Zhao, ancient mythic landscape, golden hour natural light, cosmic scale with human intimacy, dusty earth palette --ar 21:9 --style raw --s 350 --v 6`
### 模板F：清晨薄雾
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Chloe Zhao, early morning misty landscape, soft overcast light, muted earth tones, meditative stillness --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 200 --v 6`

## 三、负面提示词
`--no studio lighting, bright saturated colors, fast action, cartoon, anime, glossy commercial, dramatic hollywood lighting`

## 四、参数速查
| 效果 | 参数 |
|------|------|
| 写实自然 | `--s 180-250 --style raw --v 6` |
| 诗意化 | `--s 300-400 --style raw --v 6` |
| 宽银幕风景 | `--ar 16:9` |
| 肖像 | `--ar 3:4` |
| 史诗宽幅 | `--ar 21:9` |
