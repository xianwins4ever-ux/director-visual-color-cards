# Midjourney 提示词模板 — Ken Loach 肯·洛奇风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

---

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Ken Loach, british social realism,
natural overcast window light, muted grey-blue and warm earth palette,
working class setting, documentary film still,
socialist humanism, quiet dignity
```

## 二、场景模板（6类）
### 模板A：福利办公室
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Ken Loach, british job centre office, overcast fluorescent light, muted grey-blue palette, working class dignity, documentary realism --ar 4:3 --style raw --s 150 --v 6`
### 模板B：爱尔兰乡野
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Ken Loach, irish countryside cottage, overcast natural light, muted green and stone grey, historical drama, social realism --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 200 --v 6`
### 模板C：工人室内
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Ken Loach, working class kitchen interior, natural window light, weathered wood and stone, quiet human drama --ar 4:3 --style raw --s 150 --v 6`
### 模板D：群像对话
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Ken Loach, group of working people talking, community gathering, static medium shot, natural light, solidarity and struggle --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 200 --v 6`
### 模板E：少年成长
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Ken Loach, working class schoolboy, northern england schoolyard, overcast day, muted earth tones, youth dignity --ar 4:3 --style raw --s 180 --v 6`
### 模板F：街头抗争
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Ken Loach, protest or picket line, british industrial town, overcast grey sky, workers solidarity, muted palette --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 200 --v 6`

## 三、负面提示词
`--no dramatic lighting, hollywood style, saturated colors, hero shot, low angle power shot, cartoon, anime, 3d render`

## 四、参数速查
| 效果 | 参数 |
|------|------|
| 纪实感 | `--s 150-200 --style raw --v 6` |
| 更艺术 | `--s 300 --style raw --v 6` |
| 4:3电视画幅 | `--ar 4:3` |
| 宽银幕 | `--ar 16:9` |
| 肖像 | `--ar 3:4` |
