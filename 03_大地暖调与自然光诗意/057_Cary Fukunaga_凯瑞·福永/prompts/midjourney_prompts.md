# Midjourney 提示词模板 — 凯瑞·福永 Cary Fukunaga风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

---

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Cary Fukunaga, quiet violence,
desert sand ochre, golden hour desert light, soft dust diffusion, naturalism with poetic framing,
cinematic film look, natural lighting,
african resilience atmosphere
```

## 二、场景模板（6类）
### 模板A：自然风景
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Cary Fukunaga, desert sand ochre, golden hour natural light, epic landscape, cinematic --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 750 --v 6`
### 模板B：人物肖像
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Cary Fukunaga, intimate portrait, window light, warm skin tones, soft shadows --ar 3:4 --style raw --s 600 --v 6`
### 模板C：室内场景
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Cary Fukunaga, atmospheric interior, natural window light, burnt earth brown --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 800 --v 6`
### 模板D：史诗场景
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Cary Fukunaga, epic wide shot, long tracking shots, cinematic scale --ar 21:9 --style raw --s 700 --v 6`
### 模板E：记忆场景
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Cary Fukunaga, soft focus memory, warm golden haze, dreamlike --ar 1:1 --style raw --s 900 --v 6`
### 模板F：氛围环境
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Cary Fukunaga, atmospheric establishing shot, swamp moss green, moody --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 650 --v 6`

## 三、负面提示词
`--no flat lighting, cartoon, anime, overexposed, modern digital look, harsh shadows`

## 四、参数速查
| 效果 | 参数 |
|------|------|
| 电影感 | `--s 750 --style raw --v 6` |
| 写实 | `--s 250 --style raw --v 6` |
| 艺术化 | `--s 900 --v 6` |
| 宽银幕 | `--ar 16:9` |
| 肖像 | `--ar 3:4` |
