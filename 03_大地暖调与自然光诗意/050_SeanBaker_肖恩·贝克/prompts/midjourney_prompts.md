# Midjourney 提示词模板 — Sean Baker 肖恩·贝克风格

> 使用方法：将 `[SUBJECT]` 替换为主体描述，按需调整参数

---

## 一、通用风格标签
```
in the style of Sean Baker, florida project aesthetic,
harsh daylight sun, saturated warm orange palette,
neon pink motel, handheld vérité,
working class marginal life, cinematic film still
```

## 二、场景模板（6类）
### 模板A：烈日街头
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Sean Baker, harsh daylight sun, florida orange and pink palette, street-level handheld, motel setting, working class life --ar 21:9 --style raw --s 250 --v 6`
### 模板B：霓虹夜景
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Sean Baker, neon street night, hollywood blvd or florida strip, pink and orange neon signs, handheld vérité --ar 21:9 --style raw --s 300 --v 6`
### 模板C：人物肖像
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Sean Baker, natural light portrait, skin texture detail, street cast authenticity, warm skin tones --ar 3:4 --style raw --s 200 --v 6`
### 模板D：汽车旅馆
`[SUBJECT], in the style of Sean Baker, cheap motel exterior, bright orange walls, florida sunlight, kitsch americana aesthetic --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 350 --v 6`
### 模板E：儿童视角

`--no studio lighting, soft glow, hollywood glamour, perfect makeup, 35mm film look, wealthy setting, clean pristine environment`
