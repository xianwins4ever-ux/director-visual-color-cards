# Stable Diffusion 提示词模板 — Park Chan-wook 朴赞郁风格

> 适用于 SDXL / 1.5，配合 LoRA 使用效果更佳

---

## 一、风格 LoRA 触发词（推荐组合）

```
park_chan-wook_style, park chan-wook film aesthetic,
dark thriller film, cinematic film still
```

---

## 二、正向提示词模板（Positive Prompt）

### 模板结构
`[主体描述] + [风格标签] + [光线色彩] + [构图运镜] + [质量标签]`

### 完整示例：经典场景

```
masterpiece, best quality, ultra detailed, 8k,
cinematic film still of long corridor fight,
in the style of Park Chan-wook, dark thriller film,
opulent green-purple, deep jade green black+vengeance red,
decorative soft light, symmetrical lighting, rich saturated colors,
strict symmetrical composition, low angle shot,
the Vengeance Trilogy atmosphere, raw violence, weight of vengeance, cornered animal in corridor,
film grain, cinematic wide shot, depth of field,
dark mood, dramatic lighting
```

### 完整示例：人物肖像

```
masterpiece, best quality, ultra detailed, 8k,
portrait of a character in Park Chan-wook film style,
in the style of Park Chan-wook, dark thriller protagonist,
decorative soft light, symmetrical lighting, rich saturated colors casting dramatic shadows on face,
opulent green-purple background, golden-purple warm light key light,
the Vengeance Trilogy expression, elegant violence atmosphere,
medium close-up shot, film grain, cinematic texture
```


## 四、ControlNet 推荐组合

| 效果 | ControlNet 模型 | 强度 |
|------|---------------|------|
| 精确构图 | Canny / LineArt | 0.7-0.9 |
| 光线控制 | Depth + IP-Adapter 参考图 | 0.6-0.8 |
| 人物姿态 | OpenPose | 0.8 |
| 场景深度 | Depth Map | 0.7 |
| 色彩匹配 | IP-Adapter (参考电影截图) | 0.5-0.7 |

---

## 五、采样参数推荐

| 参数 | 推荐值 | 说明 |
|------|--------|------|
| Steps | 30-50 | Euler a / DPM++ 2M Karras |
| CFG Scale | 5-8 | 冷调悬疑风格不要过高CFG |
| Sampler | DPM++ 2M Karras | 细腻光影表现好 |
| 分辨率 | 1024×1536 (肖像) / 1536×1024 (宽屏) | SDXL 原生尺寸 |
| 高分辨率修复 | 0.5-0.7 denoising | 增强胶片颗粒感 |

---

## 六、LoRA 训练建议

如要自制 朴赞郁 LoRA：

- **训练素材**：15-20张高清电影截图（从代表作中各选3-5张）
- **触发词**：`park_chan-wook_style, park chan-wook film`
- **类别词**：`dark thriller film, cinematic film still, movie scene`
- **训练步数**：2000-3000步（SDXL）
- **重点捕捉**：色彩对比、光影特征、构图规律
